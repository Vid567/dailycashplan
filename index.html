Built around three simple promises

DailyCashPlan is an early beta. These principles guide how it is being developed.

🤝

No shame

Your numbers are information, not a personal failure. The app uses calm, practical language.

🧭

No unnecessary complexity

The aim is a useful overview and a next step, not dozens of charts and settings.

🔐

No financial-data account

Your income, expenses and debts are not uploaded to a DailyCashPlan account or server.

Real user feedback comes first.

Testimonials will only be added after beta users have shared genuine, publishable feedback. No invented reviews or placeholder praise.

Privacy and browser support

Your financial entries are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use. Clearing browser data or switching devices can remove or hide that information.

Best experience: Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone
Also supported: Current versions of Firefox and Edge; voice entry may not be available in every browser
Desktop: Supported, although the interface is designed for phones first
