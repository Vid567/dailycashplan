# Shared brand system

DailyCashPlan belongs to the same product family as PantryPlan and AI Stamp Collection Scanner. The Stamp Scanner palette is the colour source of truth.

- Primary: `#14365E`; primary controls, navigation emphasis, important links and headings.
- Secondary: `#41B6E6`; decorative accents, focus rings and large non-text highlights. Do not use for small text on white.
- Primary hover: `#0E2948`; primary pressed: `#091F38`; light accent: `#EEF5FB`.
- Background: `#F6F8FB`; surface: white; border: `#DCE5ED`.
- Cards use white surfaces, light blue-grey borders and restrained shadows. Badges use the light accent with navy text.
- Secondary buttons use the light accent with navy text. Focus uses a visible secondary-blue outline.
- Success remains green, warning remains amber, danger/error remains red, and information uses accessible blue.
- Positive cash-flow and completed states may remain green. Do not recolour chart colours when they encode financial meaning.
- Screenshots must remain accurate. Regenerate marketing graphics when obsolete branding is baked into pixels; do not tint product or lifestyle photography.
