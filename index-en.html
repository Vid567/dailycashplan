Questions before you start

Is DailyCashPlan free?

Yes. The current Browser Beta is free to use.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You enter the numbers you want to use yourself.

Where is my financial data stored?

Your plan stays on your device rather than being stored in a DailyCashPlan cloud account.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to start using the Browser Beta.

Is this financial advice?

No. DailyCashPlan is a planning tool that helps you organise the numbers you enter and explore scenarios. It does not provide personalised financial advice.
