Questions before you start

Is DailyCashPlan free?

Yes. The current Browser Beta is free to use.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You enter the amounts you want to include in your plan yourself.

Where is my financial data stored?

Your plan stays on your device rather than being stored in a DailyCashPlan cloud account.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start using the Browser Beta without creating an account.

Is this financial advice?

No. DailyCashPlan is a planning tool that helps you organise the information you enter and explore different scenarios. It does not provide personalised financial advice.
